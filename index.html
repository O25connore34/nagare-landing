01 / Scope

What we fabricate

Panels for street tuning, circuit privateers and low-volume race teams, on Japanese and international sports chassis alike.
  • A1

    Aero-corrected shells with reinforced mounting tabs at every fixing point.

  • A2

    Hoods & aero bonnets

  • A4

02 / Intake protocol

How a part gets started

Most clients never visit the workshop. Fitment is settled on references and datum points before any glass is cut.
  1. 01

    Datum & reference intake

    Send an OEM part, 3D scan data, or name a chassis we already hold tooling for.

  2. 02

    Plug & mold preparation

    Plugs are aligned to factory chassis points so panel gaps and flange depths stay uniform.

  3. 03

    Layup & gelcoat release

    Multi-layer wet layup, cured at controlled room temperature, released in black or white gelcoat.

03 / Craftsmanship

Tooling, layup, finish

Ordinary composite practice, done carefully. Orthophthalic and isophthalic resins, woven roving and chopped strand matting, high-temp epoxy tooling coats on the molds.
Reinforcement matrix
Multi-directional glass at stress corners and mounting lugs.
Surface prep
Uniform polyester gelcoat, ready for block sanding and primer.
Edge trimming
Edges scribed to meet factory liners, fasteners and shut lines.
Export packaging
Timber-reinforced crates built around the panel for freight.
